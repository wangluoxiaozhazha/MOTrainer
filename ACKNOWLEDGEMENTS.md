# Acknowledgements and project boundaries

[English](ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md) | [简体中文](ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.zh-CN.md)

MOTrainer is an independent, unofficial utility for offline Mental Omega play.

Its feature design, interaction model, and compatibility research benefited
from public documentation, observable behavior, and long-standing community
knowledge, including:

- the observable feature scope and behavior of simple Red Alert trainers;
- the observable selected-unit editing behavior of existing Red Alert tools;
- public discussion and research from the Chinese and international Mental
  Omega communities;
- the [Mental Omega official website](https://mentalomega.com/);
- the [Ares documentation](https://ares-developers.github.io/Ares-docs/);
- the [Phobos documentation](https://phobos.readthedocs.io/) and other public
  Yuri's Revenge community engineering resources;
- the project author's long-term experience with Red Alert 2: Yuri's Revenge
  and Mental Omega.

Thank you to the tool authors, documentation maintainers, mod developers, and
community contributors whose work made that knowledge available. Mention here
documents sources of influence; it does not imply endorsement, participation,
or support for MOTrainer by those people or projects.

## Independent implementation

MOTrainer was independently implemented from public information, observable
program behavior, and validation against the supported build. This repository
does not contain, adapt, or distribute binaries, source code, or assets from
third-party trainers. It also does not contain Mental Omega or Command & Conquer
game files or assets.

The public repository contains source code and user-facing technical material
needed for MOTrainer itself. Experimental logs, game samples, and third-party
programs are not release content.
